SECTION 1: OVERVIEW PAGE & INTRO (0:00 - 0:40)
================================================================================
[SCREEN / UI NAVIGATION]
1. Open browser to: https://razorpay-nexus.web.app
2. Stay on the home / Overview tab.

[VOICEOVER SCRIPT]
"Hi everyone! As AI agents evolve from simple chat assistants into autonomous 
buyers making real transactions on behalf of users, a major problem arises: 
How do merchants govern, bound, and trust AI transactions at scale? Without 
guardrails, AI agents can bypass business rules, exceed spending limits, or 
cause fraud.

Welcome to Razorpay Nexus — an Agentic Commerce Control Plane designed to 
make merchants AI-discoverable while ensuring every single AI transaction is 
explainable, bounded, policy-gated, and verified."

[BUTTON CLICK ACTION]
- Click the blue button: "Launch AI Buyer Demo" on the hero banner.


================================================================================
SECTION 2: POLICY ENGINE & BUSINESS RULES (0:40 - 1:30)
================================================================================
[SCREEN / UI NAVIGATION]
App automatically navigates to the "Policy Engine" tab.

[VOICEOVER SCRIPT]
"Here in the Policy Engine, merchants configure strict financial and catalog 
guardrails for incoming autonomous agents."

[BUTTON CLICK ACTIONS & STEPS]
Step 1: Click inside the "Max Single Order Limit" input box.
        Type/Change value from 50000 to 30000.
        
Step 2: Find the "Alcohol" category toggle switch.
        Click it to turn it OFF (Disabled).

Step 3: Click the button: "Save Policy Rules"
        Voiceover: "The policy rules update instantly in our database."

Step 4: Click the button: "Simulate Policy Check"
        Voiceover: "The engine evaluates all rules in under 2 milliseconds, 
        returning a clear ALLOW or DENY verdict."


================================================================================
SECTION 3: GEMINI AI BUYER SIMULATOR (1:30 - 2:30)
================================================================================
[SCREEN / UI NAVIGATION]
Click on the "AI Buyer Simulator" tab in top navigation bar.

[VOICEOVER SCRIPT]
"Now let's see an autonomous AI buyer in action."

[BUTTON CLICK ACTIONS & STEPS]
Step 1: Click the preset chip: "Post-workout recovery products under ₹3000"
        (Or type it directly into the prompt box).

Step 2: Click the button: "Run AI Buyer Query"
        Voiceover: "Razorpay Nexus calls Gemini 1.5 Flash to extract structured 
        intent — parsing target keywords, category, and budget bounds in real time."

Step 3: Click the button: "Generate Bounded Quote & Place Order"
        Voiceover: "Nexus issues a 10-minute cryptographically signed quote, 
        verifies it against policy rules, and executes the order through Razorpay 
        Sandbox — generating an official Razorpay Order ID."

Step 4: Click the button: "Reset Agent Chat" to clear the query screen.


================================================================================
SECTION 4: MERCHANT REVENUE DASHBOARD (2:30 - 3:30)
================================================================================
[SCREEN / UI NAVIGATION]
Click on the "Revenue Dashboard" tab in top navigation bar.

[VOICEOVER SCRIPT]
"The Merchant Revenue Dashboard gives real-time visibility into agentic 
commerce performance."

[BUTTON CLICK ACTIONS & STEPS]
Step 1: Click the time filter pill: "30 Days"
        Voiceover: "The revenue charts update dynamically."

Step 2: Hover your mouse cursor over the blue "Agentic Orders" segment on the 
        Agentic vs. Human Revenue Pie Chart.
        Voiceover: "Here we track what percentage of total revenue and order 
        volume is driven by AI agents versus direct human checkouts."

Step 3: Click the button: "Refresh Dashboard Data".


================================================================================
SECTION 5: AUDIT TRAIL & INTERACTIVE TEST LAB (3:30 - 4:30)
================================================================================
[SCREEN / UI NAVIGATION]
Click on the "Audit Trail / Test Lab" tab in top navigation bar.

[VOICEOVER SCRIPT]
"For compliance and auditing, every AI action is logged in an immutable 
cryptographic Audit Trail."

[BUTTON CLICK ACTIONS & STEPS]
Step 1: Click on the top log entry row to expand it.
        Voiceover: "Expanding any record reveals the full prompt, Gemini intent 
        JSON, policy verdict, and HMAC signature status."

Step 2: Scroll to the Test Lab section below.
        Click the test dropdown and select: "Exceed Single Order Limit Test".

Step 3: Click the button: "Execute Selected Test Case"
        Voiceover: "Our test lab executes live policy assertions to prove the 
        system blocks invalid orders."
