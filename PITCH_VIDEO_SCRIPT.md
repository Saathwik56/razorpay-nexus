## 📜 Crisp Voiceover & Button-by-Button Recording Script

### **1. OVERVIEW PAGE (0:00 – 0:40)**

* **Visual**: Open `https://razorpay-nexus.web.app` on the Overview tab.
* **🎙️ Voiceover**:  
  > *"Welcome to Razorpay Nexus — the Agentic Commerce Control Plane. Autonomous AI buyers are ordering products on behalf of users, but merchants need guardrails. Nexus makes merchants AI-discoverable while policy-gating every transaction."*
* **🔘 Button Action**: Click the blue **"Launch AI Buyer Demo"** button on the hero banner.

---

### **2. POLICY ENGINE TAB (0:40 – 1:30)**

* **Visual**: App transitions to the **Policy Engine** tab.
* **🎙️ Voiceover**:  
  > *"Here in the Policy Engine, merchants set strict business constraints for AI buyers."*
* **🔘 Button Action 1**: Click the **"Max Single Order Limit"** input box, change `50000` to `30000`.
* **🔘 Button Action 2**: Toggle the **"Alcohol Category"** switch to **OFF (Disabled)**.
* **🔘 Button Action 3**: Click the **"Save Policy Rules"** button.
  * **🎙️ Voiceover**: *"Rules are updated instantly in our database."*
* **🔘 Button Action 4**: Click **"Simulate Policy Check"**.
  * **🎙️ Voiceover**: *"The policy engine evaluates rules in under 2 milliseconds, returning a clean ALLOW or DENY verdict."*

---

### **3. AI BUYER SIMULATOR TAB (1:30 – 2:30)**

* **Visual**: Click **"AI Buyer Simulator"** tab in top navigation bar.
* **🎙️ Voiceover**:  
  > *"Now let's see an autonomous AI agent in action."*
* **🔘 Button Action 1**: Click the preset prompt chip **"Post-workout recovery products under ₹3000"**.
* **🔘 Button Action 2**: Click the **"Run AI Buyer Query"** button.
  * **🎙️ Voiceover**: *"Nexus calls Gemini 1.5 Flash to extract structured intent — parsing keywords, category, and budget bounds automatically."*
* **🔘 Button Action 3**: Click **"Generate Bounded Quote & Place Order"**.
  * **🎙️ Voiceover**: *"A 10-minute cryptographically signed quote is generated, policy-checked, and placed via Razorpay Sandbox, returning a real Razorpay Order ID!"*
* **🔘 Button Action 4**: Click **"Reset Agent Chat"** to clear the simulator.

---

### **4. REVENUE DASHBOARD TAB (2:30 – 3:30)**

* **Visual**: Click **"Revenue Dashboard"** tab in top navigation.
* **🎙️ Voiceover**:  
  > *"The Revenue Dashboard gives merchants real-time analytics on AI commerce performance."*
* **🔘 Button Action 1**: Click the time range selector pill **"30 Days"**.
  * **🎙️ Voiceover**: *"The GMV chart updates dynamically."*
* **🔘 Button Action 2**: Hover your mouse over the **"Agentic Orders"** blue segment on the **Agentic vs Human Revenue Pie Chart**.
  * **🎙️ Voiceover**: *"Notice our Agentic vs. Human Revenue chart showing exact revenue share and order count driven by AI agents versus human checkouts."*
* **🔘 Button Action 3**: Click **"Refresh Dashboard Data"**.

---

### **5. AUDIT TRAIL & TEST LAB TAB (3:30 – 4:30)**

* **Visual**: Click **"Audit Trail / Test Lab"** tab.
* **🎙️ Voiceover**:  
  > *"Every single AI decision is logged in our immutable cryptographic Audit Trail."*
* **🔘 Button Action 1**: Click on the **top log entry row** to expand it.
  * **🎙️ Voiceover**: *"Expanding any record reveals the full prompt, Gemini intent JSON, policy verdict, and HMAC signature validation."*
* **🔘 Button Action 2**: In the Test Lab section below, click the test case dropdown and select **"Exceed Single Order Limit Test"**.
* **🔘 Button Action 3**: Click the **"Execute Selected Test Case"** button.
  * **🎙️ Voiceover**: *"Our automated test suite runs live assertions against policy rules to ensure strict compliance."*

---

### **6. SYSTEM HEALTH & WRAP-UP (4:30 – 5:00)**

* **Visual**: Click **"System Health / Config"** tab.
* **🎙️ Voiceover**:  
  > *"Finally, Razorpay Nexus includes real-time diagnostics."*
* **🔘 Button Action 1**: Click **"Trigger Live Subsystem Diagnostic Check"**.
  * **🎙️ Voiceover**: *"All 6 core subsystems — Fastify API, Database, Policy Engine, Webhooks, Quote Engine, and State Machine — are verified live."*
* **🎙️ Concluding Voiceover**:  
  > *"With 51 passing tests, Docker support, and full cloud deployment on Firebase and Render, Razorpay Nexus is ready to power governed agentic commerce. Thank you!"*
